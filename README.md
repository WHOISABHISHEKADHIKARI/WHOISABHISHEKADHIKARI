## 🚀 About Me

I am an **AI Trainer, Prompt Engineer, developer, and technology educator from Nepal** focused on making artificial intelligence practical, understandable, and useful in real-world environments.

My work combines **AI education, prompt engineering, context engineering, AI agents, automation, open-source development, and digital innovation**.

I work with students, developers, professionals, businesses, educational institutions, and communities to move beyond basic AI usage and build practical AI-powered workflows and systems.

- 🎓 **AI Trainer in Nepal:** Trained **1,500+ students and professionals** in AI literacy, prompt engineering, generative AI, productivity, and workplace automation.

- 🤖 **Prompt & Context Engineering:** Design production-ready system prompts, agent instructions, reusable skills, AI workflows, and context architectures for modern LLM applications.

- ⭐ **Open-Source Contributor:** Contributor to **1,500+ AI skills and agent instructions** through the Agentic Awesome Skills ecosystem, used and referenced by developers building AI agents and automation systems.

- 🌾 **AgriTech Founder:** Founder of **Himalaya Krishi**, exploring how AI, automation, digital platforms, and smart technologies can support Nepal's agriculture sector.

- 🎙️ **AI Speaker & Educator:** Speaker on topics including **Artificial Intelligence, Prompt Engineering, Open Source, AI Agents, Digital Systems, and the future of work**.

- 🌐 **Community Builder:** Co-founder and active contributor at **DEV Community Nepal**, supporting developers through workshops, technical sessions, networking, and community-driven learning.
